01 My story

A few chapters.
Still unfolding.

Not a straight line. A story of places, people, and choosing what comes next.

✳

From building systems
to telling stories.

Along the way, I want to use what I know to make someone else’s life better.

  1. The beginningYantai, China

    Roots by the sea.

    I grew up in Yantai, Shandong. My story starts there.

  2. At eighteenShanghai, China

    A bigger city. A new direction.

    I moved to Shanghai to study software engineering at Fudan University.

  3. The next moveCalifornia, USA

    Across the Pacific.

    I earned a master’s in computer science at the University of California, Irvine.

  4. The engineering yearsFacebook

    Building things that scale.

    I spent several years working on large distributed systems at Facebook.

  5. Fall 2021North Carolina A&T

    On the other side of the classroom.

    As a visiting professor, I taught a computer science course.

  6. The startup chapterRockset → a career break

    Choosing a different next step.

    After 2½ years at Rockset and its acquisition by OpenAI, I chose a career break.

  7. Now & nextBeyond one job title

    Making room for more.

    Travel, music, and television. Including a new chapter as executive producer and actor in Boys Like Us Season 2.
